Work Skills Training & Education Program

(WSTEP)

MTN, Inc. has been awarded a grant through the ARPA Missouri's DED Workforce Development Training Grant to establish the Work Skills Training and Education Program (WSTEP).

The program provides training and education to unemployed, underemployed, and displaced adult workers in our community and twelve-county region. Participants will walk away with job-readiness skills and certificates to begin a new career path.
