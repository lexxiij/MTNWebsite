Who We Are & What Drives Us

Our Story

MTN, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that was established in 2020 during the height of the pandemic. MTN, Inc. provides food and services to persons in need.

Our organization works to decrease food insecurity and provide services to help families hardest hit by natural disasters, pandemics, and economic hardships. Although our main focus is helping people within the local community, our organization is dedicated to providing services to people throughout the state and surrounding regions.

MTN, Inc. partners with businesses and organizations to reach clients and provide services that could not otherwise be provided.

Our Mission

At MTN, Inc., our mission is to combat food insecurity, provide workforce skills and education, and offer various other essential goods and services to persons in need. The organization strives to improve lives and communities by helping to meet daily needs and helping to increase economic prosperity to ensure that people and families thrive and become productive members of the communities in which they live.
