How We're Making a Difference

Food Assistance Across the Region

Through our partnership with a local, minority-owned trucking company, MTN, Inc. is able to reach food-insecure families across a broad region of Missouri and beyond. No family should go hungry, and with the help of our partners and donors, we're making that a reality.

Workforce Development

The WSTEP program is training unemployed and underemployed adults in our 12-county region with job-ready skills in forklift operation, CDL driving, and shipyard welding. Every certificate earned is a life changed.
